Privacy policy for n8n Local

Last updated: 2026-11-09

n8n Local is a private automation tool used only by the owner of The Base View YouTube channel.

What data the app uses

The app reads only the owner's own YouTube channel analytics (for example views, watch time and subscribers of the owner's own videos) and writes them to the owner's own Google Sheet.

Sharing

No data is shared, sold or used by anyone else. The app is not offered to other users.
